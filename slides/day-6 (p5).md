# Day 6 Slide Deck: Offering All Glory to the Original Source (Principle 5)
---

## Slide 1: Title Slide
### **Sacred Community Course**

**Day 6: Offering All Glory to the Original Source**
Systematic Study of Principle 5: Uprooting pride, the danger of leadership proprietorship, and the safety of the transparent medium.

**Speaker:** HH Bhakti Dhira Damodara Swami Maharaja
**Course Text:** *Reflections on Sacred Teachings V* (Principle 5)

---

## Slide 2: Principle 5 — Offering All Glory to the Original Source
### **The Humility of the Transparent Via Media**

"Rupanuga devotees offer all glory to the original source instead of depending on their own strength."

"It is very materialistic when we have people in the devotee community who so eagerly want to get credit for their activities, and who so eagerly want to claim responsibility. Whenever Srila Prabhupada received glorification, he would react in the opposite way by giving all the credit to his spiritual master. He would explain how any successes he had achieved simply resulted from his loyal adherence to the instructions of his spiritual master."

---

## Slide 3: The Danger of Seeking Praise
### **The Neophyte Desire for Adoration**

"On the other hand, materialistic people and neophytes are so quick to let everyone know that they are the ones. Some people are so much into glorification that they get disturbed when they do not get enough praise. Therefore, when a little praise does come, they wonder why it took so long."

"When people get their Nobel prizes, their Oscars, their degrees, they say, 'It’s about time. They should have given it to me last year. Even though I had the best performance last year, at least they gave it to me this year.'"

---

## Slide 4: Identifying with the Results
### **The Hazard of Claiming Credit in Sadhana**

"Identifying with the results is very dangerous, especially for those trying to control the senses or practice celibacy. If one wants to stay fixed in celibacy but claims the results of activities, one will also have to claim the karma that comes along with that. The negative influences of false ego and pride can create havoc in a devotee’s life."

---

## Slide 5: Leadership and the Subtle Sex Trap
### **How the Desire for Distinction Breeds Agitation**

"To the degree that people, especially in positions of authority, claim the credits and accept proprietorship, they will develop more false ego. And the expression of the ego is just subtle sex, which means that too much desire for distinction, adoration, and self-glorification then leads to gross agitation and often falldown."

"People who want to claim the benefits or the achievements will later want to claim the enjoyment. They will feel that they are entitled to stimulation and additional credits because they have accepted responsibility."

---

## Slide 6: The Trap of Embezzlement and Pride
### **Viewing Assets and Accomplishments as Personal Property**

"For these reasons, we find that some religious or secular leaders are more prone to embezzlement and illicit affairs. Embezzlement happens when leaders begin to think that the assets they are handling actually belong to them. They take for themselves, keeping their own gratification in mind."

"As they deal with people, they begin to think that they are God’s gift to the world, or they begin to feel that the many accomplishments have happened due to their own strength, insight, and intelligence. The accomplishments may in fact be good, and people may tell the leader, 'You’ve helped me so much. You are so wonderful.'"

---

## Slide 7: The Slowdown of Spiritual Progress
### **The Destructive Consequences of Exploitation**

"But if the person starts to think, 'Yes, I’ve helped you; therefore, you owe me,' after awhile, they will start trying to enjoy or experience that which they oversee. We find that most people in leadership are generally cheaters because they get caught up in these sorts of gross or subtle traps. It slows down any type of progress because it involves manipulation and exploitation."

---

## Slide 8: Vaishnava Sangha
### **Heart-to-Heart Reflection**

1. Exposing the Desire for Credit: Why is it so attractive for our false ego to claim ownership over successful projects or beautiful service? How can we catch this tendency before we start demanding praise or feeling neglected?

2. The Mindset of the Caretaker: Bhakti Tirtha Swami warns that leaders who exploit those under their care face heavy consequences. How can senior devotees and managers maintain a healthy boundary to protect those who are vulnerable and open to them?

---

## Slide 9: Checking the Exploitive Tendency
### **Upholding the Sacred Trust of Leadership**

"It is natural that people who receive help from a leader will feel good about the exchanges and will want to connect with him or her more. Not only will the dependents want to get more help, but they will want to thank their leader in some way."

"If the person who offered the help tries to own the gratitude of their dependents, he or she will end up exploiting those very people who came for protection and guidance."

"It is natural for a person in a superior position to have leverage, dominance, and control over others, but such a person must be able to check the exploitive tendency as others begin to feel that kind of connection."

---

## Slide 10: The Gravity of Leadership Exploitation
### **The Warning Against Spiritual Incest**

"When a person in a superior position begins to manipulate, control, or sexually exploit those dependents, it is just like incest. When people come for help, they leave themselves open. They view the authority as a big brother or parent."

"If the caretaker then exploits those people who have become vulnerable, heavy karmic consequences will follow."

---

## Slide 11: Purity as the Major Force
### **Invoking Divine Intervention Through Sincerity**

"As devotees, we want to have the intensity and the purity that invokes divine intervention. Then, due to the devotion, other things will happen. Shortages in Krishna consciousness are maya."

"Purity is the major force. It has so much to do with simplicity, austerities, perseverance, and strong faith. As Albert Einstein once said, 'You cannot solve a problem with the same mindset that created it.' If there is a problem, we have to take a step back and be bold enough to look at ourselves first."

---

## Slide 12: Hrdaya Seva
### **An Immediate Heart Offering**

**The Sincere Mind-Fast (60 Seconds):**
Maharaja will play soft flute music and ask everyone to close their eyes and sit in complete silence. Honestly search your heart for any service or project where you are secretly seeking personal distinction, separate credit, or appreciation. Mentally gather those desires, and with absolute sincerity, lay them at the feet of the disciplic succession, praying: *“My dear Lord, I am not the enjoyer of this service. Let every ounce of credit and praise go directly to my spiritual master and Srila Prabhupada.”*

**The Mental Flower of Surrender:**
Devotees close their eyes and visualize the lotus feet of their spiritual master and the disciplic succession. Mentally gather every single ounce of success, praise, and ability you have, and place them like a beautiful, golden flower at their feet. Pray silently: *“My dear spiritual master, I did not perform this service. I have no independent strength or ability. Every success belongs solely to you. Please take all the credit and keep me as a simple servant of your servants.”*

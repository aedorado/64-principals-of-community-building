# Day 17 Slide Deck: Overcoming Deceit & Simplicity as Vaisnavism (Principles 19 & 20)
---

## Slide 1: Title Slide
### **Sacred Community Course**

**Day 17: Overcoming Deceit & Simplicity as Vaisnavism**
Systematic Study of Principle 19 (Freeing Ourselves from Duplicity) and Principle 20 (Simplicity as the True Name of Vaisnavism)

**Speaker:** HH Bhakti Dhira Damodara Swami Maharaja
**Course Text:** *Reflections on Sacred Teachings V* (Principles 19 & 20)

---

## Slide 2: Principle 19 — Overcoming Deceit
### **The Platform of True Human Consciousness**

“Srila Bhaktisiddhanta explains the degraded condition of one who is deceitful. It is better to not even pretend to be a human being because a person who is too duplicitous is not in human consciousness. Human life begins at the mode of goodness, which means that the majority of people walking the planet are not even humans.”

---

## Slide 3: Animals in Human Form
### **The Drive for Survival and Territory**

“They might have a human form, but they have the consciousness of animals because they are not pursuing self-realization. They have already taken on the mindset of animals. Everything revolves around their own lust and territory, and their entire existence is dedicated to material survival, which is animal-like.”

---

## Slide 4: Honest Facing of Material Facts
### **The Foundation of Real Honesty**

“Human life means that one must honestly face the facts of birth, death, old age, and disease. By understanding the miseries of life, one will not lead a deceitful life of just living to ensure one’s own eating, sleeping, mating, and defending. Human life begins on the platform of honesty and self-realization.”

---

## Slide 5: The Society of Cheaters and Cheated
### **The Root of Global Exploitation**

“In today’s society, people are subjected to all kinds of illusions and are expert in deceit. Most people are so dishonest with themselves, it is almost impossible for them to be truthful with others. Consequently, we end up with a society of the cheaters and the cheated, which breeds a constant atmosphere of distrust, exploitation, and violence.”

---

## Slide 6: Principle 20 — Simplicity is Vaisnavism
### **Straightforward Faith vs. Complex Struggle**

“Vaisnavism is the other name of simplicity. Simplicity does not mean foolishness or naïve. There is a difference between the two. Krishna consciousness is very, very difficult for the complex, bewildered, and faithless person, but it is very simple for the straightforward and faithful devotee.”

---

## Slide 7: The Jet Path of Mercy
### **Calamities to Burn Off Karma**

“One type of devotee has so many calamities in their life because Krishna has taken them on a jet path. Krishna wants to see if they are serious and lets the individual burn off a lot of karma right away. Therefore, many seemingly negative things happen to the devotee: they get very sick, lose a family member, or have extreme financial problems.”

---

## Slide 8: The Auspiciousness of Having Only Him
### **Uprooting Our Artificial Shelters**

“Usually, we have so many different shelters we can turn to when things go wrong. But Krishna will take all of these away. Krishna is basically putting them in a situation in which they have nothing but Him. It is auspicious. If we look at this situation with our material eyes, we will think that it is tragic, but spiritually it is highly auspicious.”

---

## Slide 9: The Danger of Spiritual Tourism
### **Changing Situations Without Changing the Heart**

“These devotees could be considered spiritual tourists. They move from temple to temple, always looking for a better environment, but they do not make any advancement. By constantly making these external changes, after awhile they just leave devotional service altogether because they haven’t really changed themselves.”

---

## Slide 10: India Making or Breaking People
### **Amplify Offenses in the Holy Dhama**

“The holy tirthas are dangerous because whatever a person does gets amplified. Have you noticed how India makes or breaks people? Some people come back from India and ‘bloop’ or even have a chronic disease for the rest of their lives. When spiritual tourists visit these places, they go in the wrong mindset, and they actually become offenders.”

---

## Slide 11: The Steps of Los Angeles (Part I)
### **The Rush to See the Spiritual Master**

“I remember one time when Srila Prabhupada was visiting the Los Angeles temple, and as usual, there was a great rush of devotees running up the steps to see him. I noticed one devotee whom many considered to be somewhat eccentric. He was just standing there, mumbling something to himself.”

---

## Slide 12: The Steps of Los Angeles (Part II)
### **The Warning of the Mumbling Devotee**

“I listened closer and heard him say, ‘I don’t know why they are all rushing so fast to see the spiritual master because they are just rushing in to make offenses.’ I thought about his observation and realized that he was right. If we approach holy people or places with an improper consciousness, we will make dhama-aparadha and in this case guru-aparadha.”

---

## Slide 13: Vaishnava Sangha
### **Heart-to-Heart Reflection (Integration Bridge)**

1. The Jet Path vs. Spiritual Tourism: Bhakti Tirtha Swami explains that Krishna sometimes takes a serious devotee on a "jet path" by systematically taking away their material shelters (health, finances, family). How can we distinguish between this purifying "jet path" and the tendency to act as "spiritual tourists" who constantly change temples or services when difficulties arise?

2. Guarding our Consciousness: Reflecting on the eccentric devotee's warning at the Los Angeles temple steps, why is approaching Srila Prabhupada or holy tirthas with a complex, competitive, or showing-off mindset considered a form of "rushing in to make offenses"? How can we cultivate the true simplicity required to receive the pure association of senior Vaisnavas?

---

## Slide 14: Hrdaya Seva
### **An Immediate Heart Offering**

**The Silent Search for Simplicity (60 Seconds):**
Close your eyes and sit in complete stillness. Bring to your mind any area of your devotional life where you are currently pretending, putting on a show, or managing your "spiritual image" before others. Honestly face this duplicity, and mentally lay down this mask of pride at the lotus feet of the disciplic succession, begging for the blessing of pure simplicity.

**The Action Vow of Simplicity:**
Identify one material entanglement or complex relationship today, and make a quiet vow to simplify it or bring transparent honesty to it. Mentally pray to Lord Caitanya: “My dear Lord, let me find my absolute shelter in simplicity. Strip away my duplicity, protect me from making offenses to Your holy places and servants, and make me a straightforward instrument of Your mercy.”

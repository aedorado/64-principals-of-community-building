## Slide 2: The Root of the Problem (Principle 16)

* **The Trap of Physical Sight & Miracles:**  
  "We suffer from an amnesia which causes us to think of ourselves as these bodies. We think it is just a matter of trying to see God or the pure devotee, or being in the physical association of great personalities who will make some miracle happen."

* **The Trap of Deflecting the Message:**  
  "Hearing does not mean simply letting sound vibration hit the ear. Someone might think: *'I am glad someone like Maharaja is telling the devotees how to act as better devotees. Although the message does not pertain to me, it is about time he tells them.'* Actually, the person who thinks in this way is the one who needs to hear it most."

---

## Slide 3: Principle 16 — Associating Through Hearing
### **Awakening from Material Amnesia**

Principle 16: "There is no way of associating with transcendence besides hearing."

"It is through hearing that we free ourselves from our current amnesia which causes us to think of ourselves as these bodies and as permanent residents of the material world. We come into contact with the spiritual world by the quality of our hearing."

"Hearing means that we internalize and imbibe the message so that we know how to act. Our service attitude affects the quality of our hearing. The quality of our service, even more than the quantity of service rendered, indicates how Krishna conscious we really are."

---

## Slide 4: **The Transmission of Consciousness**

"Sometimes we might notice that a person gives an eloquent class and quotes many stories and verses from the scripture; however, after leaving the class, we don’t feel moved to surrender. Another devotee gives a simple class, but it affects us to such an extent that we think about how to become more serious for weeks afterward."

## Slide 5: The Living Example — "Daṇḍavat-Class or Hearing Class?"
### **Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta & Śrīla Prabhupāda (Principle 16 in Action)**

> “This hearing process is very, very important. But people are not interested in hearing. They are simply busy in some other duties. My Guru Mahārāja used to say... One who was not interested in hearing, he used to call him a *daṇḍavat*-class, *daṇḍavat*-class of men. That means simply he knows how to make *daṇḍavats*, that's all. [laughter]
> 
> Anyone who will come to him, he would see whether he is a *daṇḍavat*-class of man or hearing class of man. So *daṇḍavat* is nice, but by offering *daṇḍavat*, if one does not develop the intent of hearing, *śravaṇam*, then he is not making very much progress.
> 
> As you know, because I was little interested in hearing my Guru Mahārāja, he accepted me as his disciple. He remarked this: *‘This boy is interested in hearing. He does not go away.’* Actually, I do not know; I could not understand what he was speaking in the beginning, but still I was very much interested to hear him, out of curiosity or something like that.”

*Source: Śrīla Prabhupāda (Lecture on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.2.8, Vrindavana, October 19, 1972)*

## Slide 6: Principle 17 — The Shelter of Protectors
### **Our Fortification on the Battlefield**

Principle 17: "As soon as we lose the shelter of a protector, everything around us will become our enemy and attack us."

"We know our protectors: the holy name, the Vaisnavas, the scriptures, the spiritual master, and ultimately Krishna. All of these act as catalysts to give us fortification and protection. Getting that protection also depends on having sufficient fear of the dangers."

"We must remind ourselves that we are on a dangerous battlefield and constantly need protection. Maya has some unusual tricks."

"We might have weaknesses, but we can find ways to dovetail them so that we can still stay in Krishna consciousness."

---

## Slide 7: The Living Example — The Highest Principle
### **Śrīla Prabhupāda on Saving Yourself (Principle 17 in Action)**

> **"What Is More Important?"**  
> In the early days, before I ever met Śrīla Prabhupāda, his letters were vitally instructive and enlightening. In one letter that came to Tamal Krishna Maharaja in Bury Place in 1970, there was an admonition. Tamal had asked:  
> *"What is more important: saṅkīrtana, rounds, devotional service, or our health?"*  
>  
> Prabhupāda answered:  
> **"The highest principle is to save yourself."**

*Source: Remembrance by Yogeśvara Dāsa (Memories of Śrīla Prabhupāda, Part 18, ITV)*

## Slide 8: Vaishnava Sangha
### **Heart-to-Heart Reflection**

1. **Hearing vs. Deflecting:** When sitting in class, how often does our mind deflect the instructions onto others (*"I'm glad Prabhu is hearing this, he really needs it"*) instead of allowing the sound to pierce our own amnesia? How can we cultivate a service attitude that internalizes every verse personally?

2. **Fear of the Battlefield & Dovetailing:** The text notes that getting protection depends on having "sufficient fear of the dangers." Where have we become casually complacent with material attachments? What is one weakness you can consciously "dovetail" into Kṛṣṇa's service today instead of allowing māyā to lure you away?

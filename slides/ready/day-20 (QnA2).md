## Slide 2: Q1: Antagonistic Family & Dependent Souls
### **Neutralizing Offenses & The Gravity of Karmic Debts**

“Question: Is there a reason why we took our birth in a particular family, and is it important to maintain a connection with that family if they oppose Krishna consciousness?”

“We do in fact have a karmic connection with our family members. While trying to save the world, we should also try to save our family members if we can. However, if they are so inimical that our association causes them to blaspheme, we must be cautious. The ninth offense warns against preaching to the faithless. If our family is neutral or favorable, it is our duty to preach to them; but if they are unfavorable, we shouldn’t disturb them.”

“Their antagonism often develops from lack of knowledge; they feel as if they have lost their child. We have a duty to keep them neutral. A loving word from time to time—a birthday card, a phone call, or a present—will bring satisfaction in knowing we think about them. If they knew they would receive blessings and credits due to their child’s participation, they would be extremely happy.”

“Realize what happens when a devotee goes into maya and falls prey to distractions. Not only do they procrastinate in their own spiritual life, but the devotee basically says ‘to hell’ with all those souls who are karmically depending on him or her. Understanding the amount of souls depending on our bhakti can help us whenever we feel weak.”

## Slide 3: Q1 Living Example — Śrīla Prabhupāda’s Letters on Parents (Part I)
### **Silent Respect, Patience & Avoiding Argument**

> **Respect Without Provoking Blasphemy (*Letter to Krishna Devi, Feb 20, 1968*):**  
> *“As father and mother they should be offered proper respect according to social custom, but you cannot accept their non-Godly instructions. Best thing is, to avoid misunderstanding, to remain silent without any affirmation or negation of their instructions... Don’t let them know that you do not approve your parents’ instructions... If you object to their instruction and let them know it, then they will feel sorry, sad.”*  
> 
> **Tolerance & Prahlāda’s Policy (*Letters to Gargamuni, Nov 20, 1968 & Patty Dorgan, Nov 17, 1971*):**  
> *“You must treat your father as respectful as myself; even if you are sometimes ill-treated, you should tolerate. You should follow the example of Prahlada Maharaja. His father continually tortured him in so many ways, but he never protested against his father, though he never agreed with the opinion of his father... Be very strong in avoiding meat, fish or eggs, and eventually your parents will appreciate your determination especially if you are otherwise always very kind to them.”*

## Slide 3B: Q1 Living Example — Śrīla Prabhupāda’s Letters on Parents (Part II)
### **Filial Duty, Spiritual Credit & Saving the Family Line**

> **Acknowledging Indebtedness (*Letter to Gopala Krishna, June 21, 1969*):**  
> *“The father and mother are also as good as the Spiritual Master, and we are indebted to father and mother in so many ways. So as they are dependent on your income, try to help them as far as possible, as well as spend something for Krishna also. Rupa Goswami spent 50% for Krishna, 25% for emergency, and 25% for relatives. You can also try to follow this principle as far as possible.”*  
> 
> **Invisible Spiritual Credit & Salvation (*Letters to Gargamuni & Brahmananda, 1968–1969*):**  
> *“The facilities which your father is giving you is going to his credit in Krishna Consciousness... He does not know how his Krishna Consciousness balance is increasing, but one day will come when you will see that actually your father is a good soul.”*  
> *“Just like Prahlada Maharaja—his father was atheist, and he was always inimical to his great son, but still the son was so good that he arranged for the salvation of his father... Not only your mother, but also your father and all family members will be benefited by your dedication of life for Krishna's sake.”*

## Slide 4: Q2: The Nightlife & The Enemy in the House
### **The Spectator Trap & The Danger of Unequal Yokes**

“Question: Couldn’t it sometimes be beneficial for us to watch the nightlife or witness sense gratification as a way to remind ourselves of its foolishness?”

“Do you really think somebody can continuously enjoy the nightlife and still follow the four regulative principles? It is only a matter of time. You might start out in the bar or disco, seemingly watching the Bhagavad-gita unfold, thinking yourself transcendental. However, if you remain in that association, your realizations will gradually dwindle. After awhile, you start to think like your associates because you watch them enjoy sense gratification.”

“Then the mind starts to play tricks: *‘Perhaps try a little... maybe joining will help them become devotees... maybe attracting them will make a good devotee spouse.’* It is not even easy to have a good relationship among devotees, what to speak of marrying someone with no interest in devotional culture.”

“When the devotee tries to chant, perhaps the spouse gets angry every time, considers Prabhupada’s books a waste of time, and refuses to go to the temple. It is comparable to having an enemy living in your own house.”

## Slide 5: Q2 Living Example — The Danger of Nightclubs & Bars
### **Śrīla Prabhupāda Forbids "Observing" Sense Gratification**

> **Letter to Haṁsadūta (*August 31, 1971*):**  
> When Haṁsadūta considered holding programs in nightclubs in Germany, Śrīla Prabhupāda ordered him to stop immediately:  
> *“My decision is that, at any cost, Hamburg center must be maintained and you cannot go to nightclubs. Going to nightclubs will deteriorate the quality of our transcendental chanting. Please do not do this... Our only program should be having Sankirtana on the streets and if somebody calls then at the home, and we should distribute our literatures.”*  
> 
> **Canvassing in Bars and Adult Movie Houses (*1975*):**  
> When some devotees began wearing worldly clothes to collect donations in bars and adult movie houses late at night, Prabhupāda wrote firmly:  
> *“I have read the clipping and you have also mentioned of the women wearing karmi clothes and going into sex movies and bars late at night for collecting. So this kind of canvassing should be immediately stopped.”*

## Slide 5B: Q2 Living Example — "Living in the Desert": Non-Devotee Spouses
### **Śrīla Prabhupāda’s Balanced Guidance on Household Friction**

> **1. When a Spouse Is Merely Uninterested (*Letter to Brian Fleming, March 6, 1974*):**  
> *“It is not necessary to leave your wife. The important thing is that you yourself become Krsna Conscious, she cannot check you one way or another, but you must yourself surrender to Krsna and be engaged in devotional service. We do not say that one has to leave home. Either you leave home or remain there... By and by if you are strong in your determination, she may also follow you.”*  
> 
> **2. When a Spouse Breaks Principles or Actively Opposes (*Letters to Kuśa Devī Dāsī, Feb 1975 & Disciple, Sept 1976*):**  
> *“If your husband cannot follow our principles properly, you are not to think that you should let that hamper your spiritual life. You should stay with us and cultivate spiritual life peacefully under Krishna's protection and care... Anyone who is unwilling to follow our regulated principles, you should not live or associate closely with such a person.”*  
> *“Prahlada Maharaja was only 5 years old and his father tried to kill him in so many ways. So that your husband is against Krishna is nothing astonishing, it is quite normal. So if you like you can leave him, and move into the temple of your choice.”*  
> 
> **3. Śrīla Prabhupāda’s Own Household Life (*1968 & April 26, 1973*):**  
> *“In my household life I was strictly following the regulative principles but my wife was reluctant to assist me properly. Therefore I was obliged to give up their company.”*  
> *“According to Canakya Pandita one who has no mother and the wife is not obedient, such a home is as good as living in the desert.”*

## Slide 6: Q3: Saṅkīrtana Warfare & Hostile Hearts
### **Disarming the False Ego with Genuine Compassion**

“Question: Sometimes on book distribution, a child will want a book, but the parent will refuse to let him even talk to the devotee. What should a devotee do to still try to benefit a person who shows interest in Krishna consciousness?”

“Two people, even in the same group, completely differ in their receptivity. One may have an interest, but due to peer pressure, the others convince him to disregard the book. People have free will, but peer pressure can be stronger. They have been given a chance to make contact with Krishna, but then the maya element, which is part of their own karma, might convince them to turn away.”

“In such a situation, we can try to speak with both people in mind. Sometimes if we just say a kind word to the inimical person, his or her mood will change. Often it has to do with the false ego of the individual. Once he feels that you are his well-wisher, then many times he will change right away.”

“Sankirtana is actually warfare and we must have compassion. This might be the only chance for the person to hear about Krishna; therefore, we want to act as a source of empowerment so that the person can say yes to Krishna. If we seriously try, Krishna will give us the right words to affect someone’s heart.”

## Slide 7: Q3 Living Example — Declaring War on Māyā
### **Śrīla Prabhupāda on Saṅkīrtana Warfare & Divine Empowerment**

> **1. Frontline Warfare Against Māyā (*Letters to Rāmeśvara, July 29, 1973 & Uttamaśloka, Dec 11, 1975*):**  
> *“The temple is a place not for eating and sleeping, but as a base from which we send out our soldiers to fight with maya. Fight with maya means to drop thousand and millions of books into the lap of the conditioned souls. Just like during war time the Bombs are raining from the sky like anything.”*  
> *“Just like during war time, a farm boy or ordinary clerk who goes to fight for his country on the front, immediately becomes a national hero for his sincere effort. So Krishna immediately recognizes a preacher of Krishna consciousness who takes all risks to deliver his message.”*  
> 
> **2. One Touch or Page & Words Supplied from Within (*Letters, 1970, 1972 & Feb 1975*):**  
> *“Book distribution is solid. Even if they do not read, simply by touching they are benefited. It is so much powerful... If they read even one page, the influence will be so great.”*  
> *“You mentioned that you are not yet a very bold preacher, but you will become bold, if you have got sincerity. In the beginning also I could not speak. But Krishna is within you, and when you are serving Him sincerely He will give you courage, boldness, everything... the Lord Himself supplies the light and intelligence from within the heart of a devotee who is constantly engaged in His transcendental loving service.”*

*Source: Śrīla Prabhupāda Letters (1970–1975)*

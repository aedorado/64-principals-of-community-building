# Day 7 Slide Deck: Uniting Under One Purpose (Principle 7)
---

## Slide 1: Title Slide
### **Sacred Community Course**

**Day 7: Uniting Under One Purpose**
Systematic Study of Principle 7: Becoming united with one purpose to serve Hari and cultivating Deity-centered teamship.

**Speaker:** HH Bhakti Dhira Damodara Swami Maharaja
**Course Text:** *Reflections on Sacred Teachings V* (Principle 7)

---

## Slide 2: Core Vision
### **Putting Krishna in the Center**

"Become united with one purpose and serve Hari."

"If we put Krishna in the center, we can form a great sense of unity among our associates in spite of our differences."

---

## Slide 3: Reconciling Our Differences
### **The Metaphor of the Family**

"For instance, in a family, the children may have many differences and may even fight at times, but because of their mutual love for their parents and their desire to please them, the children find ways to reconcile their differences."

---

## Slide 4: Complementing One Another
### **Building Strong Communities**

"By denying some of their own egoistic tendencies, anxieties, and problems, they find ways to help each other and even make the family stronger. As a result, they end up complementing each other."

"If we become united with one purpose in Krishna consciousness, we will create strong and powerful communities."

---

## Slide 5: Activities as Sacred Offerings
### **Uprooting Material Consciousness in Service**

"The most powerful way to achieve this goal in the Vaisnava community is to see all of our activities as offerings to Krishna. The person washing the dishes can think, “These are Krishna’s dishes.”"

---

## Slide 6: Cultivating Temple Teamship
### **The Collective Focus on the Lord**

"The people sweeping the floor, offering the arati, dressing the Deities, or painting the temple can all see their services as offerings to the Lord."

"Temples that have a very strong and powerful devotional mood focused on the Deity can then create good sadhu consciousness and guru consciousness. It will create a strong sense of teamship among the devotees."

---

## Slide 7: Vaishnava Sangha
### **Heart-to-Heart Reflection**

1. How do our daily interactions with devotees change when we consciously view our daily, menial services (like cooking, cleaning, or administrative work) as direct offerings to the Deities?

2. How can the children-and-parents family analogy help us practically resolve specific management or interpersonal conflicts in our local communities?

---

## Slide 8: Appreciating the Service of Others
### **Connecting with the Spiritual Sky**

"As devotees perform their individual services as offerings to the Deities, they can then better appreciate the service of other devotees who also focus on the Deity."

"This type of consciousness brings us more into the culture of the spiritual world."

---

## Slide 9: The Unity of the Spiritual Sky
### **Tremendous Unity with Diversity**

"In the spiritual world, everything that happens is for the satisfaction and pleasure of the Divine Couple. Consequently, there is tremendous unity with diversity as everyone is absorbed in Krishna consciousness."

---

## Slide 10: The Science of Deity Worship
### **The Science of Giving and Receiving Love**

"We can all improve in these areas. The Deity is one of the most powerful connections we have to the spiritual world."

"It is a very confidential aspect of God consciousness and a powerful science of how to give and receive love of God."

---

## Slide 11: Uprooting Impersonalism
### **Abandoning Our Lack of Accountability**

"It gives us the means to take ourselves out of the impersonal conception and forces us to abandon our lack of accountability. We have to focus, realizing that everything we do can be arranged for Krishna’s service."

---

## Slide 12: Hrdaya Seva
### **An Immediate Heart Offering**

**The Meditative Pause (60 Seconds):**
Maharaja will play soft flute music and ask everyone to close their eyes and sit in complete silence. Honestly search your heart for any daily task or service you find tedious, difficult, or boring. 

**The Visualized Flower Offering:**
Mentally place that specific chore, beautifully wrapped like a golden flower, directly at the lotus feet of the Temple Deities. Pray silently with absolute sincerity: *“My dear Lord, this service belongs entirely to You. Let me perform it solely for Your pleasure, with a pure and grateful heart.”*

# Day 6 Slide Deck: Offering All Credit & Avoiding Mixed Devotion (Principles 5 & 6)

## Slide 1: Title Slide
### **Sacred Community Course**

**Day 6: Offering All Credit & Avoiding Mixed Devotion**
Systematic Study of Principle 5 (Offering Glory to the Source) and Principle 6 (Guarding against Spiritual Tourism)

**Speaker:** HH Bhakti Dhira Damodara Swami Maharaja
**Course Text:** *Reflections on Sacred Teachings V* (Principles 5 & 6)

---

## Slide 2: Principle 5 — Offering All Glory to the Original Source
### **Following Srila Prabhupada's Example**

"It is very materialistic when we have people in the devotee community who so eagerly want to get credit for their activities, and who so eagerly want to claim responsibility. Whenever Srila Prabhupada received glorification, he would react in the opposite way by giving all the credit to his spiritual master. He would explain how any successes he had achieved simply resulted from his loyal adherence to the instructions of his spiritual master. On the other hand, materialistic people and neophytes are so quick to let everyone know that they are the ones."

---

## Slide 3: The Danger of Seeking Praise
### **The Insecurity of the Neophyte**

"Some people are so much into glorification that they get disturbed when they do not get enough praise. Therefore, when a little praise does come, they wonder why it took so long. When people get their Nobel prizes, their Oscars, their degrees, they say, “It’s about time. They should have given it to me last year. Even though I had the best performance last year, at least they gave it to me this year.”"

---

## Slide 4: Identifying with the Results
### **The Weed of False Ego and Pride**

"Identifying with the results is very dangerous, especially for those trying to control the senses or practice celibacy. If one wants to stay fixed in celibacy but claims the results of activities, one will also have to claim the karma that comes along with that. The negative influences of false ego and pride can create havoc in a devotee’s life."

---

## Slide 5: Subtle Sex and Leadership Falldowns
### **The Desire for Distinction**

"To the degree that people, especially in positions of authority, claim the credits and accept proprietorship, they will develop more false ego. And the expression of the ego is just subtle sex, which means that too much desire for distinction, adoration, and self-glorification then leads to gross agitation and often falldown. People who want to claim the benefits or the achievements will later want to claim the enjoyment. They will feel that they are entitled to stimulation and additional credits because they have accepted responsibility."

---

## Slide 6: The Trap of Proprietorship
### **Exposing the Roots of Embezzlement**

"For these reasons, we find that some religious or secular leaders are more prone to embezzlement and illicit affairs. Embezzlement happens when leaders begin to think that the assets they are handling actually belong to them. They take for themselves, keeping their own gratification in mind. As they deal with people, they begin to think that they are God’s gift to the world, or they begin to feel that the many accomplishments have happened due to their own strength, insight, and intelligence. The accomplishments may in fact be good, and people may tell the leader, “You’ve helped me so much. You are so wonderful.” But if the person starts to think, “Yes, I’ve helped you; therefore, you owe me,” after awhile, they will start trying to enjoy or experience that which they oversee. We find that most people in leadership are generally cheaters because they get caught up in these sorts of gross or subtle traps. It slows down any type of progress because it involves manipulation and exploitation."

---

## Slide 7: Checking the Exploitive Tendency
### **The Danger of Spiritual Incest**

"It is natural that people who receive help from a leader will feel good about the exchanges and will want to connect with him or her more. Not only will the dependents want to get more help, but they will want to thank their leader in some way. If the person who offered the help tries to own the gratitude of their dependents, he or she will end up exploiting those very people who came for protection and guidance. It is natural for a person in a superior position to have leverage, dominance, and control over others, but such a person must be able to check the exploitive tendency as others begin to feel that kind of connection. When a person in a superior position begins to manipulate, control, or sexually exploit those dependents, it is just like incest. When people come for help, they leave themselves open. They view the authority as a big brother or parent. If the caretaker then exploits those people who have become vulnerable, heavy karmic consequences will follow."

---

## Slide 8: Vaishnava Sangha
### **Heart-to-Heart Reflection (Integration Bridge)**

1. Subtle Ownership in Service: Bhakti Tirtha Swami warns that we carry a dangerous tendency to want credit and claim responsibility, which builds false ego and leads to severe spiritual dangers. How can we catch this "proprietorship mindset" in our daily services or when we receive praise?

2. Checking Exploitive Tendencies: How can leaders and senior devotees check the subtle, exploitive tendency to "own" the gratitude and affection of those who come to them for help, instruction, or protection?

---

## Slide 9: Principle 6 — Avoiding Mixed Devotion
### **The Surface Trap of Spiritual Tourism**

"Real love is based on chastity. People who distract themselves with so many variegated activities will not do anything with depth and quality. Some people are spiritual tourists—one day they hang out with the Buddhists, the next day they do some mantra meditation, then they go to the Catholic Church, or perhaps later explore parts of Islam. Then, after all the exploring, they practice a little of each, or perhaps they do not imbibe any of it into their lives. Such people never become deeply involved in any practice and never really commit themselves. By just remaining on the surface of each tradition, their lives become rather useless."

---

## Slide 10: The Medical Analogy of Mixed Prescriptions
### **The Need for Perseverance in One Path**

"When a person gets sick, naturally they look for treatment. But perhaps they function in this same explorative way by trying a little homeopathic remedy, then they go to an Ayurvedic doctor, next they try allopathic care, and finally they temporarily try acupuncture. Unfortunately, the person will never benefit from any of the remedies because they do not take a particular medicine long enough for it to have an effect. Even though each of these systems can have specific benefits and cures to particular ailments, if they never use a medicine properly, they will never sufficiently benefit."

---

## Slide 11: Standing Steady in One Tradition
### **Krishna’s Requirement of Unalloyed Devotion**

"Similarly, an aspiring spiritualist who never stays with a particular tradition for an extended period of time will not get the results that come from perseverance. Srila Bhaktisiddhanta makes this point clear. Krishna will not accept mixed devotion or cheating religiosity; he wants our hearts through unmotivated and uninterrupted devotion."

---

## Slide 12: Hrdaya Seva
### **An Immediate Heart Offering**

**The Silent Meditative Pause (60 Seconds):**
Maharaja will play soft flute music and ask everyone to close their eyes and sit in complete stillness. Look back at your past week of services and accomplishments. Honestly search your heart for any moments where you felt pride, demanded appreciation, or felt resentful when your work was ignored.

**The Mental Flower of Surrender:**
With absolute sincerity, visualize the lotus feet of your spiritual master and the disciplic succession. Mentally gather every single ounce of success, praise, and ability you have, and place them like a beautiful, golden flower at their feet. Pray silently: “My dear spiritual master, I did not perform this service. I have no independent strength or ability. Every success belongs solely to you. Please take all the credit and keep me as a simple servant of your servants.”

# Day 24 Slide Deck: Successful Death & Collecting the Dust of Rupa Gosvami (Principles 27 & 28)

## Slide 1: Title Slide
### **Sacred Community Course**

**Day 24: Successful Death & Collecting the Dust of Rupa Gosvami**
Systematic Study of Principle 27 (Birth Successful if Body Falls Singing Glories) and Principle 28 (Collecting the Dust of Sri Rupa Gosvami)

**Speaker:** HH Bhakti Dhira Damodara Swami Maharaja
**Course Text:** *Reflections on Sacred Teachings V* (Principles 27 & 28)

---

## Slide 2: Principle 27 — Successful Death
### **Quality Over Longevity**

> “We will not stay in this world for a long time. Only if this body falls singing the glory of Lord Sri Hari will our birth in this body be successful. ”

“Although most people are concerned with longevity, simply trying to increase the duration of life is not most important. What is most important is the quality of one’s life.”

---

## Slide 3: Exposing the Illusion of Longevity
### **The Metaphor of the Tree**

“For instance, a tree can live for hundreds of years, but this is not a sign of an advanced life. If we live a life void of devotion, we will end up wasting our rare human form.”

---

## Slide 4: The Purpose of Human Form
### **Inquiring About the Absolute**

“Every activity of the human being is to be considered a failure unless he inquires about the nature of the Absolute. Therefore those who begin to question why they are suffering or where they came from and where they shall go after death are proper students for understanding Bhagavad-gita.”

---

## Slide 5: Passing Through Safely
### **Rejecting Complacency**

“Since we will not stay in this world very long, we do not want to become complacent. Let nothing distract or disturb us because we are just passing through. Srila Bhaktisiddhanta did not say that success comes through money, fame, prestige, and so on; all such enjoyments are temporary.”

---

## Slide 6: Principle 28 — The Dust of Rupa Gosvami
### **Our Only Desired Object**

> “The only desired object in our life is to collect the dust from the lotus feet of Sri Rupa Gosvami, who fulfilled the desire of Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu.”

“We are not desireless; we are full of desires. However, we want to desire the dust of the lotus feet of the great servants of the Lord who have surrendered everything to the Divine Couple. They carry a tremendous amount of bhakti.”

---

## Slide 7: Elevating Our Desires
### **The Success of Spiritual Association**

“If we can somehow associate with these personalities, we can make our lives successful. The Caitanya-caritamrta explains that not only should we associate with such personalities, but we should collect the dust from their feet.”

“The dust of the feet of a devotee, the water that has washed the feet of a devotee, and the remnants of food left by a devotee are three very powerful substances. By rendering service to these three, one attains the supreme goal of ecstatic love for Krishna. In all the revealed scriptures this is loudly declared again and again. 

Caitanya-caritamrta, Antya-lila, 16.60-61”

---

## Slide 8: The Three Ultimate Substances
### **Ecstatic Love Unlocked**

“The dust of the feet of a devotee, the water that has washed the feet of a devotee, and the remnants of food left by a devotee are three very powerful substances. By rendering service to these three, one attains the supreme goal of ecstatic love for Krishna. In all the revealed scriptures this is loudly declared again and again.”

---

## Slide 9: Our True Identity
### **Following Srila Rupa Gosvami**

“And we especially want to take shelter of the lotus feet of Rupa Gosvami. We are rupanugas or followers of Srila Rupa Gosvami. By his mercy, we are getting a wonderful opportunity to engage in devotional service and return back to the spiritual world.”

---

## Slide 10: Vaishnava Sangha
### **Heart-to-Heart Reflection (Integration Bridge)**

1. Quality vs. Longevity: Bhakti Tirtha Swami explains that a tree can live for hundreds of years without ever advancing, and that our human birth is only successful if we leave this world singing the glories of Lord Hari. In our daily lives, how can we avoid becoming complacent or distracted by temporary concerns (like money, prestige, or comfort) and instead cultivate the urgent intensity of a soul who is just "passing through"?

2. The Three Powerful Substances: Sri Caitanya-caritamrta highlights the dust of a devotee's feet, the water that washed their feet, and their food remnants as the three ultimate substances to attain ecstatic love. How can we practically train our hearts to develop genuine, non-sentimental greed to receive these substances, and how does taking shelter of Srila Rupa Gosvami's followers protect us from the illusions of Kali-yuga?

---

## Slide 11: Hrdaya Seva
### **An Immediate Heart Offering**

**The Silent Search for Transience (60 Seconds):**
Close your eyes and sit in absolute stillness. Meditate deeply on the temporary nature of your physical body. Realize that this world is not your home and that you are merely passing through. Honestly confront any complacency, distractions, or temporary anxieties that have been draining your spiritual focus, and mentally place them at the lotus feet of Srila Rupa Gosvami.

**The Action Vow of Rupanuga Shelter:**
Chant one round of the Hare Krishna maha-mantra with intense, focused determination today, begging for the dust of Sri Rupa Gosvami's lotus feet to cleanse your heart of material desire. Pray silently: *“My dear Lord, let me not waste this rare human birth in complacency. Let me take absolute shelter of Srila Rupa Gosvami and his followers, so that when my body finally falls, my tongue and heart will be completely absorbed in singing Your holy glories.”*

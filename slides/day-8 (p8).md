## Slide 2: Principle 8 — Discussion of Hari as a Place of Pilgrimage
### **The True Definition of Sacred Space**

"Where there is discussion about Hari, that is a place of pilgrimage."

"Such discussions regarding the pastimes of the Lord sanctify any environment. A place of pilgrimage is not sacred simply due to geographical location; rather, it is sacred due to the activities that have gone on in that environment."

---

## Slide 3: The Sanctification of the Environment
### **The Blessing of Evolved Souls**

"Whether we are talking about Vrndavana, Mayapura, Jerusalem, or Mecca, when people think of such places, they are immediately reminded of the powerful spiritual activities associated with those environments. Especially when they visit, they get immersed in the mood of what has happened there."

"Furthermore, the very evolved souls who frequent such places absorb much of the negativity and also give blessings. Where there is krsna-katha and sufficient glorification of the Lord, which reminds people of God’s mercy and supreme position, that environment becomes very, very special."

---

## Slide 4: The Sacred Windows of the Temple
### **Portals into the Spiritual World**

"There are so many different types of temples all around the world—large communities with hundreds of people and small communities with only a handful of devotees—and each is a place of pilgrimage due to the constant discussions about Krishna and the spiritual world."

"Furthermore, when we enter into any of these temples, we will not see anything normal. If we look on the walls, we will not see ordinary pictures of anniversaries, birthdays, family vacations on the beach, or family members posing with large smiles. Rather, we find pictures of the Lord and His associates performing their different pastimes."

---

## Slide 5: The Temple as a Spiritual Embassy
### **The Ambassador of the Spiritual Master**

"Such pictures are like windows into the spiritual world, the temples are like spiritual embassies, and the spiritual masters are like the ambassadors."

"What happens at an embassy? If we are in New York visiting the French embassy, even though the building might be in the United States, we will enter into a different atmosphere. In the embassy, the people will speak another language, and the pictures will also be associated with their country."

---

## Slide 6: The Visa of Proper Authorization
### **The Power of Attorney to Grant Entrance**

"Furthermore, the ambassadors have the power of attorney to authorize a foreigner to visit or work in their country. People who fail to get proper certification before trying to enter another country will simply get sent back when the immigration officials do not see a visa."

"Those foreigners can explain that they went to the embassy, but didn’t fill out the necessary forms because they didn’t have time or didn’t feel that it was necessary. However, they will be told by the immigration officials to go back to the embassy in order to fill out those essential forms. What we do at the embassy will determine our status."

---

## Slide 7: Vaishnava Sangha
### **Heart-to-Heart Reflection**

1. The Spiritual Embassy: Bhakti Tirtha Swami compares the temple to an embassy and the spiritual master to an ambassador with the power of attorney. How does this shift our appreciation of the temple room? What does it mean to "obtain a visa" to enter Goloka?

2. Discussions of Hari: How can we practically make our homes and work offices into "places of pilgrimage" by changing the topics and quality of our daily conversations?

---

## Slide 8: Preparing for the Spiritual Culture
### **Acquiring Exposure to Our Eternal Home**

"Along these lines, when we enter into a Vaisnava temple, we will find many differences because it does not adhere to the culture of the material world. It is a way for one to get the proper authorization to enter into the spiritual kingdom."

"The environment is preparing us for the spiritual world by presenting an aspect of spiritual culture. If I go into the French embassy, people will speak French, and I will pick up some aspect of what I will experience when I go to France. In a very minute way, I get some exposure to the environment through the embassy."

---

## Slide 9: Unusual, Sweet, and Powerful Activities
### **Importing the Pastimes of Goloka**

"The pictures that we see of the different activities of the Lord are some of the very unusual activities that unfold in the spiritual world."

"We see pictures of the rasa dance, Krishna’s childhood pastimes, and even pictures of Krishna’s different incarnations such as Lord Nrsimhadeva, Lord Caitanya, or Lord Ramacandra to name a few. It is very foreign to what we normally reflect on in material environments because it is imported from the spiritual world, just as the holy name is imported from the spiritual world."

---

## Slide 10: The Descent of Golokera Prema-Dhana
### **The Holy Name as the Key to the Door**

"Golokera prema-dhana hari-nama-sankirtana: 'The treasure of divine love in Goloka Vrndavana has descended as the congregational chanting of Lord Hari’s holy names.'"

"It is coming into this environment so that we can associate with spiritual realities and then use it as a key for opening the door to the spiritual realms."

---

## Slide 11: Developing Appreciation for Transcendence
### **Opening the Door to the Spiritual Realms**

"As we spend more and more time in devotee communities, we will see that there are very unusual activities, but very powerful and sweet."

"As we analyze and understand them, we develop greater appreciation for the tremendous depth of knowledge that the philosophy deals with and how it helps us gain a powerful understanding of transcendence."

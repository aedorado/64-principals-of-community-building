# Day 13 Slide Deck: Following Sastra & Protecting Our Weaknesses (Principles 13 & 14)
---

## Slide 1: Title Slide
### **Sacred Community Course**

**Day 13: Following Sastra & Protecting Our Weaknesses**
Systematic Study of Principle 13 (Giving up people's opinions to hear the Vedas) and Principle 14 (Identifying and protecting our unique weaknesses)

**Speaker:** HH Bhakti Dhira Damodara Swami Maharaja
**Course Text:** *Reflections on Sacred Teachings V* (Principles 13 & 14)

---

## Slide 2: Principle 13 — Hearing Only the Vedas
### **Following the Path of True Auspiveness**

“If we want to follow the path of true auspiciousness, we should give up the countless opinions of people and only hear the words of the Vedas. Often we worry about what other people think, say, or do, which is neophyte consciousness. It means that we are too concerned about our image. If our life is our message, we do not need to be concerned about our image.”

---

## Slide 3: Pleasing Krishna Only
### **Moving Beyond Whimsical Speculation**

“If we are trying to please everyone, we will please no one and end up frustrated. We ultimately want to please Krishna. We are not interested in people’s brands of speculation or their sometimes whimsical perceptions.”

---

## Slide 4: Disturbing Maya
### **Developing Real Spiritual Potency**

“We want to know what is prescribed by the Vedas and then act accordingly, even if we become infamous as a result. If deviant and sinful people see us as a disturbance, that is good. We will definitely disturb maya if we have any potency. If we are not disturbing maya, it means that we do not have that much potency.”

---

## Slide 5: Principle 14 — Accepting the Auspicious
### **The Core Principle of Surrender**

“Whatever is auspicious should be desired. In the process of surrender, a devotee should accept things favorable for discharging devotional service and reject things unfavorable. We have to look at our own lives and activities in order to determine what is favorable.”

---

## Slide 6: The Cherry Pie Devotee
### **The Subtle Power of Material Weaknesses**

“In the early days when I was a brahmacari traveling on the BBT Library Party, a very nice devotee who also traveled with us had an interesting weakness. He loved cherry pie, and his mother was so clever. On holidays and especially on his birthday, she would send a little box with a cherry pie along with sweet words. However, one time the cherry pie came, and the next day he was gone.”

---

## Slide 7: Old Attachments and Regrets
### **Obstacles on the Path of Devotion**

“Each person has his or her own unique weaknesses. One person might hanker for an old boyfriend or girlfriend while another person might lament about all he could have achieved if he hadn’t been chanting Hare Krishna for ten years. All of these attachments and regrets can present obstacles on the path of devotional service.”

---

## Slide 8: Maya's Sudden Attack
### **Testing Our Spiritual Seriousness**

“Maya knows our greatest weaknesses, and it is amazing how she works. Whenever we try to become more serious, maya will attack more than ever. For instance, perhaps that old boyfriend or girlfriend from the past will show up. Suddenly a devotee gets a phone call from a person who he or she hasn’t heard from in fourteen years. And the phone call comes just at the point when the devotee really wants to become more serious in spiritual life.”

---

## Slide 9: The Boxing Match of Maya
### **The Strategy of the Spiritual Warrior**

“Just as in boxing or in any other sport, the opponent will take advantage of a person’s weak spot and strike in that area. Maya has a duty to see if we are ready to go back to Krishna, and by testing our weaknesses, she will determine our readiness. Therefore, we use the strategies of a spiritual warrior by simply being honest.”

“In some cases, we may have to avoid certain environments and associates from the past, especially in the neophyte stage, and accept new behaviors for protection such as extra austerities. We sometimes lose devotees who might even be doing a service such as book distribution because they get tempted by sense gratification while they are at a concert or on the street due to their past associations.”


---

## Slide 10: Vigilance Against Maya
### **Bracing Ourselves for the Test**

“If we always consider that maya is just around the corner, constantly waiting for a chance to attack as soon as our guards are down, we will be vigilant and maya will not cause as much trouble. We have to honestly look at ourselves and take notice of some of our stronger and weaker areas.”

---

## Slide 11: Vaishnava Sangha
### **Heart-to-Heart Reflection (Integration Bridge)**

1. Image Management vs. Pleasing Krishna: Bhakti Tirtha Swami teaches that being overly concerned with our image and trying to please everyone leads to neophyte frustration. How can we practically train our minds to focus strictly on what is prescribed in the Vedas, even if our choices make us unpopular or infamous in our social circles?

2. Identifying and Bracing Our Weaknesses: Bhakti Tirtha Swami compares Maya's attacks to a boxer who strikes at an opponent's exact weak spot. What are the spiritual benefits of being brutally honest with ourselves about our unique, material weak spots, and how can we practically "brace" those vulnerable areas before Maya attacks?

---

## Slide 12: Hrdaya Seva
### **An Immediate Heart Offering**

**The Silent Weakness Audit (60 Seconds):**
Close your eyes and sit in complete stillness. Bring to your mind the one material weakness, attachment, or past regret that you find most difficult to let go of—the "weak spot" where you are most vulnerable to Maya's sudden attacks. Honestly and humbly acknowledge this weakness before the parampara, without any defense or denial.

**The Action Vow of Protection:**
Write down this specific weakness on a private slip of paper. Below it, write down one practical boundary, safeguard, or mentorship consultation you will set up today to protect this weak spot. Walk away from your screen and place this folded paper directly under your home altar or Deity as a sacred, confidential promise to Srila Prabhupada and the disciplic succession.

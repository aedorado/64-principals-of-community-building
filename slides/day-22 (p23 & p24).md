### **Sacred Community Course**

**Day 22: Practice vs. Preaching & Diminishing Family Attachment**
Systematic Study of Principle 23 (Preaching Without Practicing is Karma-Kanda) and Principle 24 (Diminishing Household Attachment Through Service)

**Speaker:** HH Bhakti Dhira Damodara Swami Maharaja
**Course Text:** *Reflections on Sacred Teachings V* (Principles 23 & 24)

---

## Slide 2: Principle 23 — Preaching vs. Practicing
### **The Platform of True Example**

> “Preaching without practicing is nothing but a ritual of karma-kanda.”

“Preaching without practicing is nothing but a ritual of karma-kanda. Real preaching is through example. Speaking the philosophy without honestly being an example of the message falls into the category of karma-kanda according to Srila Bhaktisiddhanta.”

---

## Slide 3: Aligning Speech and Behavior
### **Moving Past Mechanical Rituals**

“A proper devotee’s vacana (speech) should be in line with his pracara (behavior), and his sadacara (good behavior) ultimately is his real preaching. Transcendental knowledge cannot be taught or accepted mechanically.”

---

## Slide 4: Cultivating Causeless Knowledge
### **The Scientific Result of Bhakti**

“By rendering devotional service unto the Personality of Godhead, Sri Krishna, one immediately acquires causeless knowledge and detachment from the world. Sincere service immediately activates the highest realization.”

---

## Slide 5: Focus on the Essence
### **Shedding the Superficialities**

“The authentic devotee is not interested in superficialities or externalities, but is focused on the essence. Therefore, his preaching includes practice as he shows by example what is to be done and how it is to be done.”

---

## Slide 6: The Compassion of Example
### **Preaching as an Act of Love**

“This is a sign of not only his faith and genuineness, but is also a sign of his compassion for others because he wants to show them clearly what is the proper understanding and proper behavior.”

---

## Slide 7: Principle 24 — Diminishing Attachment
### **The Household as a Temple**

> “If one just serves the Supreme Personality of Godhead and His devotees, his attachment to his household will diminish.”

“If one just serves the Supreme Personality of Godhead and His devotees, his attachment to his household will diminish. When someone functions as a grhamedhi, he or she is so attached to the whole idea of household life and all that comes as a byproduct.”

---

## Slide 8: Exposing the Grhamedhi Mindset
### **The Illusion of Personal Territory**

“Such householders view everything as their territory and they act as proprietors of all they survey. However, if a householder can channel some of that energy into serving the devotees and the Supreme Lord, then the person will not have this grhamedhi consciousness.”

---

## Slide 9: The Mundane Atmosphere
### **Struggling for Survival**

“When householders center their lives around themselves and their individual needs, it creates a mundane atmosphere of just struggling for survival and sense gratification.”

---

## Slide 10: Transforming Greed and Lust
### **The Alchemy of Devotion**

“However, when they use what they have in Krishna’s service, it becomes devotional service, which is beneficial for everyone. Devotional service transforms greed and lust into selflessness, compassion, and love.”

---

## Slide 11: Vaishnava Sangha
### **Heart-to-Heart Reflection (Integration Bridge)**

1. Preaching through Example: Bhakti Tirtha Swami states that speaking spiritual truths without practicing them reduces preaching to mere karma-kanda. In our daily dealings, how can we detect and overcome the subtle tendency to speak or preach beyond our actual level of realization, and instead ensure our behavior (sadacara) matches our speech (vacana)?

2. From Grhamedhi to Grhastha: Grhamedhi consciousness is defined as viewing everything in household life as our territory and acting as the proprietors. What are some highly practical ways for married and family-focused devotees to center their homes, finances, and relationships around serving Krishna and the devotees, thereby naturally diminishing selfish attachments?

---

## Slide 12: Hrdaya Seva
### **An Immediate Heart Offering**

**The Silent Search for Proprietary Pride (60 Seconds):**
Close your eyes and sit in quiet reflection. Bring to your mind your home, your family, your finances, or your personal possessions. Honestly face any areas where you are harboring a grhamedhi mindset—possessively claiming these things as "your territory" or demanding personal enjoyment from them. Mentally lay down your proprietary pride at the lotus feet of the disciplic succession.

**The Action Vow of Sincerity:**
Identify one material resource, home asset, or financial comfort that you have held back or used solely for personal gratification. Make a firm, quiet promise to use or offer that specific resource for the pleasure of the Deities or the service of a peer devotee today, praying to Lord Caitanya to transform your greed into pure, unalloyed love.

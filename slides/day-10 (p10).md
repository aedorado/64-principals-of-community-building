# Day 10 Slide Deck: The Art of Self-Rectification & Love-Finding (Principle 10)
---

## Slide 1: Title Slide
### **Sacred Community Course**

**Day 10: The Art of Self-Rectification & The Science of Love-Finding**
Systematic Study of Principle 10: Uprooting critical speech, codifying behavior as a cry for love, and resolving the mirror of repeating tests.

**Speaker:** HH Bhakti Dhira Damodara Swami Maharaja
**Course Text:** *Reflections on Sacred Teachings V* (Principle 10)

---

## Slide 2: Principle 10 — My Advice is: Do Not Criticize Others
### **The Great Lesson of Self-Rectification**

"My advice is, do not criticize others. Try to rectify yourself."

"What does this mean? Does it mean that people should do whatever they want without restriction? Does it mean that whatever happens is alright? No. We definitely are to scrutinize because we want to understand the scriptures. We want to apply the scriptures in our own lives, and we want to see other people applying them as well."

---

## Slide 3: Meticulously Observing the Environment
### **The Duty of Leaders to Guard Against Illusion**

"One of our main duties in devotional service is to keep out the maya, and one of the specific duties of leaders is to keep out the maya. To keep out maya, a person has to be very meticulously observing the situation and dealing with issues sufficiently."

"However, instead of faultfinding, a person should go around love-finding, trying to see how to help make improvements and how to better serve others."

---

## Slide 4: Exposing the Motives of the Fault-Finder
### **The Ego's Artificial Elevation**

"The person who focuses so much on the faults of others is really trying to put others down so that they can artificially elevate themselves."

"They are really saying, 'You eat too much, but look at me; I don’t eat nearly as much. You sleep too much, but look at me; I am so austere... You are not a serious devotee, but I am very serious.' People who fault-find have this mood."

"In reality, they are just trying to broadcast how wonderful they are by showing someone else’s weaknesses and issues. We don’t want to fault-find; we want to love-find in order to try to help."

---

## Slide 5: Codifying Behavior as a Cry for Love
### **Transforming Our Reaction to Hostility**

"One way to remain loving in an unloving or challenging environment is to always codify the behavior of others as a cry for love."

"If we see a person’s behavior as an attack, to that same degree we feel that we have to fight them back or defend ourselves. Nobody can hurt you unless you let them hurt and distract you."

"However, a person who is a deep lover is able to maintain that consciousness even when other people have different moods. He or she realizes that every person is ultimately looking for love."

---

## Slide 6: Transforming Situations of Envy and Anger
### **Viewing jealousy and Agitation Positively**

"We can transform the situation by seeing enviousness as a cry for love. We can see jealously as a cry for love. We can see anger as a cry for love. We can see fear as a cry for love."

"This person who seems envious or jealous is actually feeling that the love I am getting is not coming to them. They feel left out. They see me as a thief who has taken what should have belonged to them. In these cases, we can try to see what we can do to help supply the love."

"When someone gets angry with us, see it as a cry for love. They feel angry because something has caused them to feel uncared for and unloved."

---

## Slide 7: Vaishnava Sangha
### **Heart-to-Heart Reflection**

1. Exposing Ego Broadcasting: Bhakti Tirtha Swami says that fault-finders are simply trying to broadcast how wonderful they are by highlighting other people's mistakes. How can we catch this subtle ego trick before we open our mouths to gossip?

2. Codifying a Cry for Love: Can you share a practical experience where responding to an angry or envious devotee with patience, kindness, and "love-finding" completely transformed a hostile situation into an opportunity for deep healing?

---

## Slide 8: The "Black Eye" Metaphor of Ego-Fights
### **Why Both Sides Lose in Dualistic Conflict**

"Keep in mind that if someone is angry with you and you are angry with them, everyone loses."

"Consider a fight between two people. The so-called winner may boast that he really beat up the other person, but he himself has a black eye, a swollen jaw, and ripped clothes."

"Although he may boast that he won, actually they both lost. One may have lost a little more, but they both suffered in the process. When we get into the mood of adhering to the low consciousness of other people, then we hurt ourselves because we allow them to distract us from our real purpose."

---

## Slide 9: The Message in Repeating Challenges
### **Understanding the Mirror of Our Mind**

"If we find that a problem or situation keeps repeating itself, there is a reason and it usually means that we haven’t transcended the problem or understood the message. There is a message for us because there are no accidents."

"We have chances to learn in so many ways. If someone who we deal with on a one-on-one basis is confronting us with an issue, we have to try to understand the dynamics of that. What is Krishna telling us about ourselves and about the field of activities? Once we get the message, we will find that we open up a whole new chapter."

---

## Slide 10: Carrying Our Own Diseased Minds
### **Why Changing Environments Does Not Cure Us**

"Sometimes devotees will go from one temple to another and constantly change services, but wherever they go, they find that a similar type of problem confronts them."

"If in our lives we find this happening, it means that the biggest problem is in our own minds because we carry the same mind with us regardless of where we go."

"It is not the environment that we need to change; rather, we need to work on ourselves."

---

## Slide 11: Exposing Fake, Passive-Aggressive Tolerance
### **The Subtle Expression of False Ego**

"Some people who are tolerant let you know that they are tolerant. They let you know that you are a nonsense who really gets on their nerves, but they will tolerate you in spite of all your mistakes."

"In actuality, they are not really tolerating because they are actually angry and irritated. They simply want to let you know how low you are so that they can show how advanced they are."

"This mindset obviously does not indicate real tolerance; rather, it is just another form of false ego."

---

## Slide 12: Amplifying the Good in Others
### **Overshadowing the Negative Through Love**

"If we are really tolerant, then we will look at ourselves to see how we can make a difference. We will tune into a person’s good qualities and try to reinforce those instead of feeding their negativity."

"Even though another person might have so many issues, we should look more at his or her good qualities. We want to see how we can appreciate each devotee and encourage them based on all of their good characteristics. Then we can amplify and reinforce the good so that it can later overshadow the negative."

---
